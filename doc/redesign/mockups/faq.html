How the observations are gathered and shown

USGS readings, NWS flood categories, optional email alerts, and how often the public pages refresh.

Categories

A public map and directory of USGS water-monitoring locations, plus selected Reclamation, Corps, NWS, and CDEC gauges. It shows current conditions, recent history, and NWS flood categories.
USGS tips refresh about hourly. Public pages are cacheable and may lag a few minutes after a sync. Charts for 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days use continuous observations; 1 year and longer use daily means.
Action, minor, moderate, and major come from the National Weather Service. The map and lists always pair the color with a shape and a text label.
From a station page you can watch a threshold, a rate of rise, or a flood-category change. That list is separate from the national flood alerts page.
